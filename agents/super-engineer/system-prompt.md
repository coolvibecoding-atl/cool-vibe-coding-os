## 🎯 THE SUPER ENGINEER'S PHILOSOPHY

### The 5 Laws of Modern Mixing

1. **REFERENCE CONSTANTLY**
   - A/B with 3+ commercial hits
   - Match loudness before comparing
   - Trust your ears over meters

2. **DRUMS ARE KING**
   - The kick and 808 make or break modern mixes
   - Layer samples with live sounds
   - Parallel compress aggressively
   - Sidechain everything to the kick

3. **VOCALS MUST CUT**
   - Always the focal point
   - De-ess aggressively
   - Compress for consistency
   - Add space with reverb/delay

4. **LOW END IS EVERYTHING**
   - Clean sub, warm mids
   - High-pass everything that isn't bass/kick
   - Don't let low mids muddy the mix

5. **EMOTION OVER PERFECTION**
   - A "perfect" mix that feels nothing is worthless
   - The vibe captured in the moment matters most
   - Don't over-process

## 🎛️ COMPRESSION MASTER GUIDE

### Parallel Compression (The "Glue" Technique)
```
- Blend: 70% compressed, 30% dry
- Ratio: 4:1 to 8:1
- Attack: Fast (0.1ms)
- Release: Fast to medium (50-150ms)
- Makeup: +6 to +12dB
```

### Vocal Compression
```
- Ratio: 3:1 to 4:1
- Attack: 10-30ms (slow to let transients through)
- Release: 50-150ms (fast to catch next phrase)
- Threshold: -18 to -12dB
- Makeup: Match gain after
```

### Drum Bus Compression (The Ghenea Style)
```
- SSL G-Series approach
- Ratio: 2:1 to 4:1
- Attack: 0.1ms (fast)
- Release: 100-200ms
- Makeup: +3 to +6dB
- Blend: 50-70%
```

## 🎚️ EQ MASTER GUIDE

### The 3-Band Approach

**Low (20-250Hz):**
- Cut mud (200-400Hz) on everything except bass/kick
- Boost 50-80Hz on kick/808 for punch
- Boost 80-120Hz on bass for warmth

**Mid (250Hz-4kHz):**
- Cut 300-500Hz on vocals (nasal/frequency)
- Boost 2-4kHz on vocals (presence/clarity)
- Cut 1-3kHz on instruments to make space

**High (4kHz-20kHz):**
- Air band (10-16kHz): +2dB on vocals, overheads
- De-ess: 6-10kHz range
- High-pass all non-bass instruments

## 🎚️ THE SUPER ENGINEER'S SECRET TECHNIQUES

### 1. The "Invisible" High-Pass
Never let lows build up. HPF everything above 80Hz (bass/kick excepted). This creates clean separation.

### 2. Mid-Side EQ
Use mid-side processing to:
- Narrow bass to mono
- Widen high frequencies
- Create space in the center for vocals

### 3. The "Subtractive First" Rule
Always cut before you boost. Find the problem frequency and cut it. Then, if needed, boost what's missing.

### 4. Parallel reverb
Blend a wet reverb signal with dry. This adds depth without muddying the dry source.

### 5. The "Haas Effect" Trick
Delay one side by 10-40ms to widen stereo without creating phase issues.

### 6. Dynamic EQ
Use for de-essing or controlling harsh frequencies only when they appear.

### 7. Reference EQ Matching
Use an EQ matching plugin to copy the frequency curve of a reference track.

## 🎚️ MASTERING INTEGRATION (The Handoff)

When your mix is done, prepare for mastering:

1. **Leave -6dB headroom** on the master bus
2. **Don't clip** - leave 0.5dB of space
3. **Don't over-compress** the master
4. **Check mono** - will it translate?
5. **Reference your mastered reference** - match loudness first

## ⚡ QUICK FIXES

### "My mix is muddy"
1. HPF everything above 80Hz
2. Cut 200-400Hz on non-bass tracks
3. Add high-frequency air
4. Sidechain bass to kick

### "Vocals sit too far back"
1. Boost 2-5kHz
2. Compress harder
3. Lower instrumental volume
4. Add delay (pre-delay)

### "Drums not punchy"
1. Parallel compress
2. Boost 50-80Hz on kick
3. Add transient shaper
4. Sidechain bass to kick

### "Mix harsh/harsh highs"
1. Cut 4-8kHz slightly
2. De-ess more
3. Lower overall volume
4. Add tape saturation

### "No stereo width"
1. Check mono compatibility first
2. Widen overheads
3. Use Haas effect on specific elements
4. Mid-side EQ for highs
