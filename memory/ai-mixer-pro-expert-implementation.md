## CORE MIXING PRINCIPLES (From Experts)

### 1. Serban Ghenea Method (The Invisible Mix)
**Source:** "Mix Masters: The Secrets of Serban Ghenea's Sound"
**Key Insights:**
- "Invisible mixing" - make it sound perfect without being over-processed
- Focus on translation across all playback systems
- Less is more - don't over-process
- Reference constantly to commercial releases

### 2. Jaycen Joshua's Low-End Technique
**Source:** "Jaycen Joshua's SECRET Lowend Mixing Technique"
**Key Insights:**
- Kick & bass relationship is CRITICAL in hip-hop/trap
- Sidechain compression for bass to kick
- Parallel processing for weight without mud
- Sub bass management

### 3. Leslie Brathwaite - Rap Vocals
**Source:** "How to Mix Rap Vocals | Leslie Brathwaite (Pharrell Williams)"
**Key Insights:**
- Vocal chain: EQ → Compression → De-ess → Reverb/Delay
- Frequency-specific compression
- Automation for vocal presence
- "Dry" vs "Wet" vocal decisions

### 4. Thomas Tillie Mann - Trap/Hip-Hop
**Source:** "Mixing Masterclass: Hip Hop & Trap Mixing"
**Key Insights:**
- 808 management is key
- Snare/hi-hat relationship
- Vocal effects chain for modern trap sound
- Loudness optimization

### 5. Luca Pretolesi - Loudness & Mastering
**Source:** "HOW TO Mastering Levels & Gain Staging"
**Key Insights:**
- Proper gain staging throughout the chain
- LUFS targeting: -14 LUFS for streaming
- Limit ceiling at -0.3dB to -0.5dB
- True peak awareness

## IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

### Phase 1: Core Processing (MVP)
1. **Stem Separation** - Isolate vocals, drums, bass, melody
2. **Auto-Leveling** - Balance track levels
3. **Basic EQ** - Subtractive EQ for clarity
4. **Compression** - Gentle dynamic control
5. **Mastering** - LUFS normalization

### Phase 2: Expert Features
1. **Genre Detection** - Identify hip-hop, pop, rock, EDM
2. **Style Matching** - Reference-based processing
3. **Vocal Processing** - Professional vocal chain
4. **Low-End Enhancement** - Jaycen Joshua technique

### Phase 3: Advanced AI
1. **Reference Learning** - Compare to professional mixes
2. **Style Transfer** - Mix like specific engineers
3. **A/B Comparison** - Before/after with reference

## GENRE-SPECIFIC PRESETS

### Hip-Hop/Trap
- Aggressive 808s
- Punchy snare
- Vocal presence
- -7 LUFS integrated

### Pop
- Balanced frequency spectrum
- Loudness: -8 LUFS
- Bright top end
- Vocal-forward mix

### R&B
- Warm low-mids
- Smooth compression
- Reverb/delay tails
- -9 LUFS integrated

### EDM
- Heavy compression
- Excited highs
- Loud: -6 LUFS
- Sidechain everything

## QUALITY METRICS

### What Makes a Mix "Professional":
1. **Clarity** - Every element audible
2. **Balance** - Proper level relationships
3. **Translation** - Sounds good everywhere
4. **Dynamics** - Alive, not squashed
5. **Frequency** - No muddy lows, harsh highs

### AI Quality Checks:
- RMS vs Peak ratio
- LUFS measurement
- Stereo width analysis
- Frequency spectrum balance
- Phase correlation
